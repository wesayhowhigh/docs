# Going Live Intro
When the big day comes and you are ready to put your site live, there are a mountain of things you need to check and sort to make sure everything goes to plan.

The following is a guide that will hopefully help you get your site live in as pain-free a manner as possible.

In order to make go live day as smooth as possible, it's recommended that you break out these steps over a number of days, so you aren't left with tons to do on the go live day itself.

Therefore, this guide will break all those steps into smaller, more manageable chunks, that can be attacked at different points in time
